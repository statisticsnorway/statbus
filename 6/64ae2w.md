## Goal
On an enterprise, legal unit or establishment page, the side column shows that unit's notes and, expandable, the notes of the units it is tied to by primary: the enterprise, its primary legal unit, and that legal unit's primary establishment, each with a link to its page.

## Purpose
An enterprise's information is derived from its primary legal unit, and a legal unit's activity often from its primary establishment, so a note about the data is often written on another unit of the chain. Today each page shows only its own note (n9nyvd), and a user must visit every unit to find it (owner 2026-11-18, enterprise Veridit AS on no.statbus.org).

## Principle
The primary chain is the reading order: enterprise, primary legal unit, primary establishment. Notes stay one text per unit (n9nyvd); this task shows them together, it does not merge or copy them. The owner designs the layout before it is built.

## Relevant details
Owner's notes 2026-11-18 on the enterprise page (screenshots in the session): (1) the side column's notes box gets the other notes of the chain, as links or expandable, the primary legal unit's first and below it the primary establishment's; (2) the box 'The information for this enterprise is derived from the primary legal unit Veridit AS. If you need to update this information, update the primary legal unit.' may move up into the header (Veridit AS, Manage settings for Veridit AS, Enterprise); (3) the Links and external references page (the enterprise, its legal unit, its establishment, with * marking primary) may not need notes of its own if the side column makes the chain clear. Code: app/src/components/unit-notes/unit-notes-box.tsx (n9nyvd's box); app/src/app/enterprises/[id]/@primaryUnitInfo/default.tsx (the derived-from box); public.unit_notes (one row per unit).

## Goal
On an enterprise, legal unit or establishment page, the side column shows that unit's notes and, expandable, the notes of the units it is tied to by primary: the enterprise, its primary legal unit, and that legal unit's primary establishment, each with a link to its page.

## Purpose
An enterprise's information is derived from its primary legal unit, and a legal unit's activity often from its primary establishment, so a note about the data is often written on another unit of the chain. Today each page shows only its own note (n9nyvd), and a user must visit every unit to find it (owner 2026-11-18, enterprise Veridit AS on no.statbus.org).

## Principle
The primary chain is the reading order: enterprise, primary legal unit, primary establishment. Notes stay one text per unit (n9nyvd); this task shows them together, it does not merge or copy them. The owner designs the layout before it is built.

## Relevant details
Layout, owner 2026-11-18 and 2026-11-20 (answers in the notes): (1) the side column's notes box shows the primary chain, always in the order enterprise, legal unit, establishment, and looks the same on all three pages; the unit whose page you are on is open (its note shown and editable), the other two are collapsed and opened by hand. (2) The enterprise page's derived-from box ('The information for this enterprise is derived from the primary legal unit Veridit AS. If you need to update this information, update the primary legal unit.') moves into the enterprise header as one line, 'Derived from the primary legal unit Veridit AS' with the name a link (answer 4, option c). The legal unit and establishment pages no longer have it (839f84c98: the notice beside Save replaced it). (3) The Links and external references page shows no notes. Code: app/src/components/unit-notes/unit-notes-box.tsx (n9nyvd's box); app/src/app/enterprises/[id]/@primaryUnitInfo/default.tsx (the derived-from box); public.unit_notes (one row per unit).

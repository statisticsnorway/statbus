Every producer publishes immediately through `worker.publish_base_change_wakeup`, which always updates the durable pending row, and the collector locks that row before its drain snapshot. Before, a producer that found the flag already TRUE took no row lock, so a collector clearing the flag could lose a committed change's wakeup. A forced three-connection interleaving (gate, collector, ordinary producer) shows the stable definitions ending with the change logged and `has_pending` FALSE, and this candidate ending with `has_pending` TRUE and one queued collector.

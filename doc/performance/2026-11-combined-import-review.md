- [ ] **Small-history isolation:** Review original commit `37dbbc11c` and its rebased equivalent in the candidate. Confirm `import_norway_small_history` uses `--isolated` and review its candidate regression evidence below.
- [ ] **Whole-unit packing:** Review [batch assignment](../../migrations/20261107162052_processing_batches_hold_whole_units.up.sql). Dependency groups hash into 16,384 import slots, then whole occupied slots are packed toward the row target. An oversized slot stays whole. Import slots do not promise derive locality. Confirm a founding unit spanning batches forces serial dispatch.
- [ ] **Eligibility and ordering:** Review [concurrent dispatch](../../migrations/20261107164600_concurrent_independent_import_batches.up.sql). Parallel processing is limited to eligible new legal units with new enterprises or formal establishments with resolved parents, insert-only rows, intact founding units and known handlers. Mixed updates, reused targets, informal establishments and custom handlers fall back to serial. A clean historical file can still analyze as mixed. Separate jobs remain one at a time and holistic steps remain serial.
- [ ] **Failure, replay and barrier:** Confirm a batch's transactional domain writes, row states and counter update roll back on failure, diagnostics and failed job state survive the handler rollback, and failure propagates through fan-in. Committed siblings can remain committed. A committed batch replay does no work, and holistic processing and finishing wait for successful children. Review the [design](../import-concurrent-batches-design.md) and candidate regression evidence.
- [ ] **Wakeup and collector race:** Review [wakeup migration](../../migrations/20261107180400_defer_import_batch_change_wakeup.up.sql). Batch scopes defer publication, not change logs or integrity checks, until transaction tail. Ordinary writes publish immediately. Every publisher locks the durable pending row, and the collector locks it before its drain snapshot, preventing a flag clear from losing a committed wakeup. Review forced two-connection interleaving and rollback evidence in the [design](../import-concurrent-batches-design.md). This is not proof of host-crash recovery.
- [ ] **Power groups and parity:** Review [analysis](../../migrations/20261107133923_power_group_analysis_joins_either_end_of_a_relationship_by_equality.up.sql) and [processing](../../migrations/20261107164500_power_group_processing_merge_cardinality.up.sql), including cycle-tail root repair (original `68559913a`).

relayed the operator's report, and answered questions; this document records that.

accommodation when they do not.

## Deviations — each is a ticket, per this card's own rule

1. **Nobody can tell who upgraded the box.** The account owner could not determine
   from any surface that his colleague was the one who instigated the upgrade — the
   row records every *when* and no *who*, and the shared unix account erases identity
   before the product sees it. → **STATBUS-317** (upgrade actor attribution).
2. **The progress log speaks to its author, not its operator** — five findings from
   reading this single log: an indirect read-only-window line whose parenthetical is
   the real message; "listen-loop goroutine" jargon; two adjacent disconnect lines an
   operator cannot tell apart (the zoom-out-then-in principle violated); no
   backup-completed line, so completion is indistinguishable from abandonment; and
   "Recovering an interrupted upgrade" printed on the *planned* post-swap handoff,
   reading like a logic error when nothing was interrupted. None are blockers.
   → **STATBUS-318** (upgrade log narrative pass).

## Goal
The quality step of Deriving reports shows a name, a readable result, and for a small edit takes seconds, not minutes.

## Purpose
A small edit must not wait minutes for the quality step: the dashboard and search counts lag until it finishes.

## Principle
Follow the other report steps: take the payload's changed units and range, and split large work into concurrent children (structured concurrency) like derive_statistical_unit_facet. A full rebuild stays for full-range payloads.

## Details
Owner test 2026-11-26 on Norway: a one-unit edit spent 2m32s of the 2m52s report phase in derive_quality. worker.derive_quality ignores its payload and calls public.quality_finding_derive() (MERGE over all of quality_finding_def). COMMAND_LABELS in app/src/atoms/worker_status.ts had no derive_quality entry, and p_info nests the per-rule counts under 'findings', which the Info column printed as [object Object]. Changed units are known per cycle as public.statistical_unit_facet_dirty_hash_slots (cleared by statistical_history_facet_delta_reduce).

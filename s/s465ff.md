## Goal
Each statistical variable chooses how a legal unit's total combines its own value with its establishments': sum or max.

## Purpose
Owner 2026-11-26: Norway reports employees on both levels, so summing counts people twice, while most countries report each value on one level and need a plain sum. SSB found one fixed rule too confusing; the choice is per variable and can change after install.

## Principle
One enum public.lu_es_rollup ('sum','max') on stat_definition, default sum (the rule of every released version). Enterprises and power groups always sum their legal units. Changing it recalculates totals in the background. The quality checks follow the rule. Visuals: the total is Σ; what follows '=' shows how it was made (Σ 369 = 364 + 5, Σ 364 = max(364, 5)); final visuals and naming in the admin page are decided with the owner before building the UI.

## Details
Today on master (not released): public.jsonb_stats_covers always takes the larger level (ca6dk5, migration 20261124114235), used in timeline_legal_unit_def_for. Norway's Employees and Turnover would be set to max. Admin page wording to agree: 'How a legal unit's total is made: Sum / Max'.

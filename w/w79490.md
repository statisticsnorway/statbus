## Goal
A field worker can photograph a unit (a shop on the street, a sign) to recognise it on the next visit. The photo is cropped to a square and stored small, it stays dated to the period it was taken for like any field, and a photo that no period of any unit shows any more is deleted by the database.

## Purpose
Owner 2026-11-19 15:35. Today an upload may be up to 4 MB uncropped, and removing or replacing a photo leaves it in public.image forever (6 orphans on the test slot after the 2026-11-12 walkthrough, vtgjj0). Photos are expected to be few, so the limit is what keeps the database from filling up.

## Principle
The database guarantees the cleanup and the size limit, whichever path writes; the app only makes the photo small enough to pass. A later year may get its own photo; history keeps the old one while a period still refers to it.

## Relevant details
Cleanup: deferred constraint triggers on legal_unit and establishment (UPDATE OF image_id, DELETE) remove the old image at commit when no row of either table refers to it, so sql_saga's row splits in the same transaction never delete a photo still in use. Size: CHECK on public.image lowered from 4 MB to 256 KB (fleet has no images on dev, ma, demo, no; checked 2026-11-19). App: the image field crops to a square, scales to 512 px and saves JPEG; a failed save removes the photo it uploaded; the unused updateLegalUnitImage/deleteLegalUnitImage/updateEstablishmentImage/deleteEstablishmentImage actions are removed.

## Goal
We know how to give Norway's 1M legal units and 792K establishments coordinates from their BRREG address using Kartverket's official data.

## Purpose
Owner 2026-11-22: lat+lon for Norwegian units from Kartverket's official API and the address, as an alternative to Erik's files (r39dqb).

## Principle
Official, open sources (Kartverket, CC BY 4.0); a unit whose address cannot be matched falls back to its postcode's centre, and the match quality is recorded.

## Details
Kartverket's Adresse REST-API (https://ws.geonorge.no/adresser/v1/, sok and punktsok) is open without registration but returns at most 10 000 hits per query and is not meant for complete datasets; Kartverket points to the Matrikkelen - Adresse download on geonorge.no for those. So the full register means: download Matrikkelen - Adresse, match BRREG's street, number, letter and postcode locally, and use the API only for spot checks. Competes with r39dqb (Erik's files); the owner chooses.

## Goal
On a phone-width screen a report's content uses the whole width, with the REPORTS side menu folded away.

## Purpose
Owner 2026-11-28 13:57 tried /reports/map at phone width: the side menu kept 224 px of the 422, the heading wrapped one word per line and the map and its table were cut off at the right.

## Principle
One pattern for all reports, in reports/layout.tsx, not per page. Follow the top bar's existing ☰ fold rather than inventing a new control. Wide screens keep the side menu as it is.

## Details
reports/layout.tsx renders <aside className='... min-w-56'> beside the <section>, at every width. The fold could be a ☰ button or a row of links above the content. The map page itself stacks map above table below md (ppvxg2, 2026-11-28).
